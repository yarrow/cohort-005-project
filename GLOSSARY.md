# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase, made within the Refund Window. A Platform Admin approves or declines it. One purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one can be undecided at once.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request no Platform Admin has decided yet. While it is open, the learner cannot view the course.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has accepted. Approving it is what leads to a Refund, and the learner no longer has the course.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request a Platform Admin has turned down, giving the learner a reason. It has no Refund, the learner has the course again, and they may raise a new Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Denied, Rejected

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner after their Refund Request is approved. It is always the full amount they paid. A declined Refund Request has no Refund.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund a Platform Admin has approved where the money hasn't been confirmed as sent yet.
_Avoid_: Pending (for a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been confirmed as sent back to the learner.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved, because the decision hasn't changed.

### Team Licences

**Team Licence**:
A purchase of a course made for a team, giving it a number of Seats to hand out.
_Avoid_: Bulk purchase, Group licence

**Seat**:
One place on a Team Licence, handed out as a code that a team member claims to get the course.
_Avoid_: Coupon, Voucher

**Revoked Seat**:
An unclaimed Seat that can no longer be claimed because its Team Licence was refunded.
_Avoid_: Voided, Cancelled, Expired

### People

**Platform Admin**:
A member of the Cadence team who runs the platform. Only a Platform Admin decides Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), Staff

**Team Admin**:
A member of a customer's team who manages that team's Seats.
_Avoid_: Admin (on its own), Owner
